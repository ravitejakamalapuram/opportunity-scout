## Decision signals

Prioritize work that is most likely to improve one or more of:

1. customer evidence;
2. paid demand;
3. product usage/retention;
4. distribution;
5. revenue;
6. reduction of meaningful technical or platform risk.

Avoid optimizing vanity metrics when stronger evidence is available.

## Next-5-hours rule

Each daily run should identify the highest-value use of the next available work block, considering the entire portfolio rather than only new ideas.
